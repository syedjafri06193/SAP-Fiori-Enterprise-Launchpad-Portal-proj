# User Onboarding & Training – BINARY

## 1. Access Request Process

```
Manager request ─▶ Role owner approval ─▶ SoD check (Security) ─▶ Provisioning ─▶ Welcome e-mail
```

| Step | Owner | SLA |
|---|---|---|
| Request role(s) and company code(s) in the access request form | Line manager | – |
| Approve business need | Business role owner | 2 business days |
| User-level SoD analysis; mitigation if needed | Security | 1 business day |
| Assign derived role(s), set validity | Security / provisioning | 1 business day |
| Send welcome note with launchpad URL and quick reference | Service desk | Same day |

**Movers** get new roles and lose old ones on the same day. **Leavers** are locked automatically from the HR feed.

## 3. End-User Quick Reference

### Getting in
- Open **https://fiori.binary.example/sap/bc/ui2/flp** in Chrome or Edge. You are signed in automatically with your company account.

### Finding your way
| I want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| See my work area | Click your space at the top (e.g. **Accounts Payable**) |
| Open an app | Click its tile or link on the page |
| Find another app I'm allowed to use | Profile menu → **App Finder**, or the search icon |
| Keep favorite apps handy | Add them to **My Home** |
| See approvals waiting | Bell icon (notifications) or **My Inbox** tile |
| Change language/theme | Profile → **Settings** |
| Get help on a screen | Profile → **Help** (Web Assistant) |
| Report a problem | Profile → **Contact Support** (includes a screenshot and technical details automatically) |

### Tips
- Use the browser's back button or the **Home** icon to return to your page.
- If something looks outdated, press **Ctrl+F5**.
- Don't share launchpad links containing personal data in e-mails.

### If something doesn't work
Please tell the service desk:
1. The app (tile) name and the space you clicked it from
2. What you did and the exact error message
3. The time it happened
4. A screenshot

## 5. FAQ

**Where are my old groups?**  
Groups are replaced by pages designed for your role. Add favorites to My Home.

**A tile I used before is gone.**  
Check App Finder. If it's not there, it isn't part of your role; ask your manager to request access if you need it.

**Why do I see different tiles than my colleague?**  
Tiles depend on your role and company codes.

**Can I rearrange my page?**  
Pages are maintained centrally so everyone gets updates. Use My Home for your personal layout.
