# Spaces and Pages Strategy – BINARY

## Overview

BINARY adopts SAP’s recommended **Spaces and Pages** model to deliver a consistent and scalable user experience across financial roles.

---

## Why Spaces and Pages

- Improved usability over traditional groups
- Clear task-based navigation
- Better performance and layout control

---

## Design Guidelines

- Pages organized by business process
- High-frequency tasks prioritized
- KPI and analytical tiles positioned at top
- Administrative functions separated

---

## Sample Page Layout

- Section 1: Daily Financial Tasks
- Section 2: Reporting and Analytics
- Section 3: Administrative Functions

---

## Benefits

- Reduced cognitive load
- Faster task execution
- Consistent experience across departments
